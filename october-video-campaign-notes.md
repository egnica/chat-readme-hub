### Video 3 of 4: What’s holding you back from video?

**Purpose:** Address several barriers rather than making camera discomfort the entire topic.

**Script draft accepted as a working version:**

Want videos for your business, but keep putting it off?

Maybe you don’t know what to say. We’ll start with the questions your customers ask and build a script around what they need to know.

Don’t want to be on camera? We can show your work while you tell the story through voiceover, or bring in a professional narrator.

Worried about finding the time? I’ll handle the planning, filming, and editing. You bring your knowledge of your business.

**Production notes:**

- Show examples that support the concern being addressed.
- Voiceover can use the client’s own recorded voice.
- Professional narration is an optional third-party service.
- Planning where a video belongs does not include publishing or website implementation in the $500 package.
