# Fall Newsletter — Social Hub Concept

## Goal

Create the first newsletter for people in the CRM.

The newsletter should feel personal and conversational rather than like a traditional marketing email. It can introduce a format that continues going forward:

- One main thing I’m building
- One question / idea I’m exploring
- A few things from the blog
- A featured song that gives the issue some personality

---

## Theme

### October / Fall

Fall is here, and I’ve been spending a lot of time building out the next phase of my business and experimenting with new tools.

The main project to feature in this issue is the **Social Content Hub / Gignovate**.

There is also an opportunity to loosely connect the October theme with social media and the idea that:

> Somebody’s always watching — especially on social.

Keep the reference subtle rather than making the entire newsletter Halloween-themed.

---

## Featured Song

### “Somebody’s Watching Me” — Rockwell

Michael Jackson sings the recognizable hook.

This fits October without using something as obvious as “Thriller,” while also connecting naturally to the social media topic.

Possible recurring newsletter element:

**This Issue’s Soundtrack**

Could link to a small splash page on my website rather than directly to a streaming service.

The splash page could include:

- Song
- Artist
- Album art / visual
- Short note about why I picked it
- Link to listen

This could become a recurring personality element in future newsletters.

---

## Opening

Personalize using the recipient’s first name from the CRM.

Possible direction:

> {{firstName}},
>
> Fall is here, and I’ve been spending a lot of time building — both new tools and the next version of what I want my business to become.
>
> And since somebody’s always watching — especially on social — one of the bigger things I’ve been working on is a new way to manage all of that.

The wording still needs refinement, but the introduction should be short.

Avoid a generic “Hi” or overly formal newsletter introduction.

---

## Main Feature — Social Content Hub

Feature the new Social Content Hub / Gignovate project.

Explain it in accessible terms instead of going deeply technical.

Possible topics:

- Managing content from one place
- Facebook and Instagram publishing
- Scheduling
- Media management
- Eventually expanding into additional social platforms
- Building something that connects content, websites, email, social and other digital tools

Link to the blog post about building the Social Content Hub once the post is ready.

### CTA

**Read what I’m building →**

This should visually feel like the button style already being used in the email system.

## Quick Question

Create a visually separate callout box.

### QUICK QUESTION

I’ve also been thinking about what it would look like for a business to have its **own private AI knowledge system**.

A company already has a huge amount of useful information spread across things like:

- Blog posts
- Website content
- PDFs
- Internal documents
- Product information
- FAQs
- Processes and procedures

What if an AI could use that information as its own knowledge source and become something built specifically around that business?

This could eventually become a service/product I offer businesses.

Keep this section exploratory. The goal is to determine whether people find the idea interesting — not sell it yet.

### CTA

**I’m interested →**

Use a `mailto:` link.

The button could pre-populate something like:

**Subject:** Private AI Idea

Potential body:

> Nick — I’d be interested in hearing more about this.

Make responding almost effortless.

   - “Somebody’s Watching Me”

   - Social Content Hub / Gignovate

   - Keep it personal

---

## Tone

The newsletter should feel like:

> Here’s what I’ve been building and thinking about lately.

Not:

> Here are the services I would like to sell you.

It should give people a reason to stay connected, learn what I’m working on, and occasionally start a conversation.

Over time, this can become a recurring communication with everyone in the CRM rather than a traditional promotional email.
